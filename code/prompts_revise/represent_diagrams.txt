DIAGRAM READING
Read the supplied original figure with its caption, footnotes, panel labels, and legends. Follow arrowheads to determine directed source and target; do not infer direction from visual placement. Single-headed arrows between modeled constructs can represent structural paths. Double-headed covariance/correlation arrows, arrows to measurement indicators, and measurement-error paths are ineligible.
An arrow from a construct whose arrowhead lands on another path can represent moderation of that path; establish the focal predictor and outcome before assigning the moderator. The interaction coefficient belongs to that moderated relation, not automatically to a moderator-to-outcome main effect. Do not infer moderated mediation or an indirect estimate merely from the existence of component paths.
Separate construct labels from R² values, error variances, and other node annotations. Numbers inside or beside a node are not path coefficients unless explicitly identified as such. Associate an edge label with the edge it annotates. Use panel and group legends before assigning model/group scope. Apply canonical names after reading the original labels.
Use a star legend only within its stated scope. A legend mapping a symbol to p<0.001 supplies that p bound, and a legend defining a symbol as significant at α supplies p="<α", for each edge carrying that symbol; stars without a legend supply no p-value. Apply the shared significance rule. Missing stars, dashed edges, or colors alone do not imply nonsignificance or control status unless defined by the figure.
Keep explicit hypothesized paths in this image with unreported statistics null. Read tested paths with their reported statistics. Merge repeated representations within this input only when they clearly describe the same relation. Other images are combined during aggregation through the same context mapping; do not claim to have inspected them.
Use null for unreadable statistics. Do not guess endpoints or mediator/moderator roles. If the image is a table, use TABLE READING; if it shows no eligible structural relations, return no relations from it.
