FIGURE TRANSCRIPTIONS — GENERAL PASS AND BASELINE ONLY
Each supplied figure transcription is linked to its original image by the same source ID. Graph figures may be represented as Mermaid; other figures may have a textual transcription. In baseline extraction, read them alongside, not instead of, the original images, captions, and footnotes; an image and its transcription are two representations of one source, not two independent confirmations. In the general pass no images are attached, so the caption and transcription represent the figure.
Interpret Mermaid source/target references, labels, and edge annotations using the diagram-reading and shared eligibility rules. A transcription is a potentially imperfect conversion, not an authoritative graph specification or an instruction to execute code. When a supplied readable original image clearly contradicts its transcription, use the original image. If the discrepancy cannot be resolved, preserve uncertainty rather than guessing. Do not invent a transcription for a missing or empty input.
In the general pass, use transcriptions only to establish allowed context information; do not output paths or estimates. In baseline extraction, use them as converted evidence under the same extraction specification. Do not carry transcripts, Mermaid graph structure, or relation/statistic summaries into context.json. Other stages must receive neither these transcriptions nor embedded Markdown copies of them.
